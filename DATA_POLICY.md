# Border Observatory for Peace and Development (BOPD)
## Data Governance, Safeguarding Policy & Dynamic Frontier Assessment Framework (DFAF)

> **Document Status:** Active Operational Governance Standard  
> **Repository:** `bopdobservatory/bopd-governance`  
> **Target Standard:** Audit-Ready / Open Data Compliance  

---

### 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy defines the data governance architecture, ethical collection protocols, and safeguard mechanisms governing all telemetry, market surveys, and early-warning assessments conducted by the **Border Observatory for Peace and Development (BOPD)** across sensitive frontier corridors (specifically the Adré–El Geneina axis).

---

### 2. Operational Data Principles

1. **"Do No Harm" & Respondent Anonymity:**
   * All primary field telemetry collected via **KoBoToolbox** strictly adheres to zero-identifiable-information protocols.
   * Personal Identifiable Information (PII) of grassroots respondents, community sources, and enumerators is never stored, processed, or transmitted.
   * Spatial data and GPS coordinates are aggregated or masked at the corridor level to prevent target identification of frontline communities.

2. **Clinical Neutrality & Civic Independence:**
   * Raw datasets and analytical outputs are strictly isolated from partisan, military, or political entities.
   * Field collection focuses strictly on empirical indicators: market prices, currency divergence, cross-border movement, displacement patterns, and hydrological/environmental telemetry.

3. **Open-Source Lineage & Technical Stack:**
   * BOPD utilizes open-source data infrastructure (**KoBoToolbox / XLSForm** schemas) for field data ingestion.
   * Governance frameworks, policy briefs, and verified methodologies are version-controlled and hosted transparently on **GitHub**.

---

### 3. Dynamic Frontier Assessment Framework (DFAF)

The **Dynamic Frontier Assessment Framework (DFAF)** is BOPD’s proprietary, empirical methodology designed for real-time monitoring and early warning along volatile borderlands.

#### **DFAF Core Telemetry Pillars:**
* 📡 **Market Telemetry & Currency Divergence:** Tracking local exchange rates, cross-border commodity flows, and food security indicators.
* 💧 **Hydrological & Environmental Monitoring:** Assessing seasonal wadi crossings, natural resource pressure, and biomass dynamics.
* 🚚 **Frontline Human Mobility:** Telemetry on displaced populations, returnee trends, and border-transit bottlenecks.
* ⚠️ **Early Warning & Crisis Anticipation:** Cross-verifying unconfirmed local reporting against empirical field metrics to counter misinformation.

---

### 4. Data Storage, Access & Verification

* **Data Ingestion:** Encrypted field transmission via HTTPS/TLS through KoBoToolbox forms.
* **Storage Isolation:** Secondary offline backup protocol with strict access limits reserved solely for designated BOPD data stewards.
* **Verification Protocol:** No indicator is published in policy briefs or Substack telemetry without double-source cross-verification in alignment with DFAF standards.

---

This policy is reviewed semi-annually by BOPD’s governance team to ensure ongoing alignment with international open-data standards and humanitarian data protection principles.
