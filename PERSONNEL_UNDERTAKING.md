# Border Observatory for Peace and Development (BOPD)
## Personnel Code of Conduct & Operational Integrity Undertaking

> **Notice:** This undertaking is a mandatory compliance requirement for all prospective personnel, researchers, consultants, and field data enumerators prior to formal onboarding and task deployment.

---

### 1. Statement of Principles & Institutional Mission
I, the undersigned, acknowledge that the **Border Observatory for Peace and Development (BOPD)** is an independent, civic research initiative operating across fragile borderland ecosystems. I understand that the Observatory's primary mandate is to provide **unbiased, frontline field telemetry, open-source analytics, and strategic policy insights** to support humanitarian response, crisis anticipation, and regional peacebuilding.

---

### 2. Core Operational Commitments & Undertakings

By signing this document, I solemnly declare and pledge adherence to the following standards:

1. **Absolute Civic Independence & Clinical Neutrality:**
   * I commit to maintaining complete impartiality and clinical neutrality in all field data collection, analysis, and reporting activities.
   * I will ensure strict data isolation from any political, military, armed, or partisan factions.
   * I shall not utilize my position or association with the Observatory for personal, political, or commercial gain.

2. **Data Integrity & Open-Source Lineage:**
   * I undertake to uphold the highest standards of data accuracy and transparency, relying exclusively on empirical field telemetry and grassroots patterns rather than unverified rumors or speculative assumptions.
   * I agree to adhere to the Observatory's open-source data architecture (including **KoBoToolbox** and **GitHub** protocols) while protecting data sovereignty and respondent confidentiality at all times.

3. **Safeguarding, Security & "Do No Harm":**
   * I pledge strict application of the "Do No Harm" framework in all field interactions, prioritizing the safety, dignity, and rights of local communities, vulnerable groups, and displaced populations across border corridors.
   * I shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding sensitive field telemetry, mapping protocols, and respondent identifiers to prevent any harm to frontline communities or colleagues.

4. **Information Integrity & Counter-Misinformation:**
   * I will actively guard against the dissemination of rumors or destabilizing information, ensuring that all reported indicators undergo verification in accordance with the Dynamic Frontier Assessment Framework (**DFAF**).

5. **Professional Conduct & Institutional Representation:**
   * I agree to represent the Observatory with professional integrity, cultural sensitivity, and ethical discipline in all engagements with local communities, international partners, and institutional entities.

---

### 3. Acknowledgment & Affirmation

I affirm that I have read, understood, and agreed to abide by all the terms set forth in this Undertaking. I understand that any deliberate violation of these principles—particularly regarding neutrality, data falsification, or unauthorized disclosure of sensitive field data—will result in immediate termination and institutional review.
